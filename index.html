Privacy Policy — Redloop (App)

Last updated: May 20, 2026

Summary

- The Redloop app collects a minimum of personal data necessary for core functionality: account data (if you sign in via FirebaseAuth), purchase/subscription status via StoreKit/Apple, and notification identifiers.
- The app does not show third‑party ads and does not sell personal data.
- The app does not collect health data and does not provide UGC chat or moderation services.

1. Data controller

- Owner: Serghei Rodomaniuc
- Privacy contact: deadlines.app@gmail.com

2. Data we may collect and purpose

- Account data: name and email — if you create an account or sign in via Firebase Authentication. Used for sync, account recovery and personalized settings.
- Purchase information: Payment is processed by Apple. The app receives confirmation of purchases/subscriptions, product identifiers and entitlement status to manage access.
- Technical and log data: usage times, crash logs, device model and iOS version — for diagnostics and to improve the app.
- Notifications: device token/identifier for local/remote notifications if you grant permission.

3. Legal basis

- Processing is based on your consent (e.g., account creation, notification opt‑in) and/or performance of a contract (managing purchases and granting paid features).

4. Sharing

- We do not sell or share personal data with advertisers.
- Firebase Authentication (Google) may process account data — third‑party service subject to its own privacy policy.
- Any analytics services are optional and will be disclosed and opt‑in if added.

5. Retention and deletion

- Data is retained as long as necessary to provide the service and to comply with legal obligations. You can request deletion of your account and personal data.

6. Security

- We employ reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data, but no transmission over the Internet is completely secure.

7. Children

- The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under this age.

8. Encryption

- The app does not use non‑exempt encryption; `ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption` is set to `false` in Info.plist.

9. Your rights

- You can request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of your personal data, and lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

10. Changes

- We may update this policy; the date at the top indicates the last update.

11. Contact

- Privacy contact: deadlines.app@gmail.com
